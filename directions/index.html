Directions

ICASSP 2027 will be held in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. The WASAI satellite workshop will take place at the main conference venue. Detailed venue information, including the specific room assignment for the workshop, will be announced closer to the conference date.

For travel information, accommodation recommendations, and local transportation options, please visit the ICASSP 2027 official website.

If you have any questions about reaching the workshop venue, please contact the organizers.
